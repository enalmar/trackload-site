Trackload

Privacy

Last updated 12 November 2026

Trackload collects nothing, and has no server to send anything to. This page says where your training is kept, and what leaves your phone only when you turn it on.

What is collected

Nothing. Trackload has no account, no sign-up and no login. It contains no analytics, no crash reporting, no advertising identifiers and no third-party SDKs of any kind. There is no server behind it, so there is nowhere for your training data to be sent.

Where your training lives

Everything you log — your sets, your weights, the program you are running and your app settings — is stored in a single file inside the app's own container on your iPhone. Other apps cannot read it.

iCloud

Optional, and off unless you turn it on. When it is on, that same file is copied into your own private iCloud container, so your history survives deleting the app and reaches your other devices. It goes to Apple, under your Apple Account. The developer has no access to it and no way to read it.

With backup on, Trackload also keeps a folder called Trackload in your iCloud Drive, so you can use your plan and history on a Mac. It holds the program you are running as a text file (program.txt), each program you have made, your current weights (lifts.csv) and your logged sets and sessions as CSV files. You can see it in the Files app and in Finder, and anything that can read your iCloud Drive can read it. The app reads back program.txt, and only to show you an edit for review. The folder stays after you delete the app, until you remove it.

If you used iCloud backup in Trackload 1.0, the folder also holds that version's backup, rack-training.json: a copy of your training as it stood in 1.0. Trackload 2.0 reads it so that sessions logged on a phone still running 1.0 come across, and never changes or deletes it.

Changing your plan with an LLM

Copy for LLM puts your plan, your current weights and your recent training on the clipboard as text. Trackload sends nothing to any LLM or any other service: you choose whether to paste it, and where. A reply you paste back is read on your phone and applied only when you confirm it.

In-app purchase

Five changes made with an LLM are free, and one optional, one-time purchase unlocks more. The purchase is made through the App Store and handled entirely by Apple; the developer receives no payment details and no information about who you are. The app checks the purchase with Apple's own signed records on your device.

To keep count of the free changes, the app stores one number — how many you have used — in your iCloud Keychain, alongside your passwords. It stays there if you delete the app, so the free changes are not handed out again on reinstall. It contains nothing else and never leaves Apple's services.

Health

Optional, and off unless you turn it on. With your permission, finished sessions are written to the Health app as strength training workouts, so they appear in Fitness alongside everything else you do. A saved workout includes an estimate of the energy it used, when a body mass is on file.

When you turn on Health writing, Trackload also asks to read your body mass from Health, to estimate a session's energy cost. It is used in memory only. It is never written to the file on your phone, never copied to iCloud, and never transmitted anywhere.

Version 1.0 also offered an optional heart rate strap. When one was connected, a saved workout included its heart rate, and the app read resting heart rate, date of birth and the highest heart rate on record in the last two years to work out heart rate zones, in memory only. Version 2.0 removes the strap and reads none of these.

Bluetooth

Trackload 2.0 and later do not use Bluetooth. Version 1.0 could pair with a standard Bluetooth heart rate strap, if you chose to connect one, and remembered which strap you last used on your device and nowhere else.

Children

Trackload collects no data from anyone, so it collects no data from children. It contains no advertising, no chat and no user-generated content. Its one optional in-app purchase is made through the App Store, where Apple's family controls, such as Ask to Buy, apply.

Deleting your data

Delete the app. With iCloud backup off, your log goes with it. With iCloud backup on, the copy in your iCloud container remains until you remove it, which you can do in Settings → your name → iCloud → Manage Account Storage, and so does the Trackload folder in iCloud Drive, which you can delete in the Files app. Inside the app, Settings → Erase everything clears the log without deleting the app.

Either way, the count of free LLM changes used stays in your iCloud Keychain. It is a single number and identifies nothing about you.

Changes to this policy

If this policy changes, the date at the top of this page changes with it. Trackload collects nothing today, and any change to that would come with a new version of the app and an updated App Store privacy label.

This website

It reports totals such as which pages are viewed, which sites send visitors here, and visitors’ countries and browsers. Nothing from the app is involved.
