Trackload

Quiet and competent. It shows you the number and the rule that produced it, then gets out of the way.

iPhone, iOS 18 or later. Free, with no in-app purchases.

What it does

The programs, whole

Twelve programs: linear progressions, upper/lower and push/pull/legs splits, and percentage-driven waves. Each one carries its own percentage table rather than an approximation of it. Where a structure could not be represented faithfully, it was left out instead of guessed at.

The next weight, already decided

Open the app and the session is laid out: every lift, every set, every weight, rounded to plates you actually own. When a lift stalls, the app tells you the weight held and why. It does not console you.

Logging that gets out of the way

A set is one tap. Add RPE if you want it, or leave it off. A rest timer runs on the Lock Screen and in the Dynamic Island, so the phone can go back in your pocket between sets.

What the numbers say

Weekly hard sets and tonnage per muscle, with the anatomy map lit by volume. Intensity distribution. Push to pull and quad to hamstring balance. Estimated one-rep max per lift, Epley or Brzycki. Progressive load charts for every exercise.

The progression engine

This is the part that decides what you lift next. Six schemes run underneath the twelve programs, and the app will always show you which one is running and what it did. A percentage program runs off a training max rather than a true max, so the prescribed weights stay hittable on a bad day.

SchemeHow the next weight is decided
LinearA fixed increment every session, until a miss.
Linear with AMRAPAs above, but the last set is taken as far as it will go, and a big set earns a double jump.
Double progressionReps climb inside a range first; weight only moves once the top of the range is met on every set.
Three-week wavesPercentages of a training max across three weeks, then the max itself moves.
High-volume wavesThe same idea with more sets per week, for programs built around accumulated work.
TieredA heavy tier, a volume tier and an accessory tier, each progressing on its own terms.

Privacy

No account. No analytics. No third-party SDKs. The whole thing is one file on your phone.

iCloud backup and Health export are both optional and both off until you turn them on. Read the privacy policy.

Built for one thing

Barbell strength. No cardio scoring, no recovery index, no readiness number, no streaks to keep.
