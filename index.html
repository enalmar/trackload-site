AI coaching, from the AI you already use.

Trackload turns any LLM into a strength coach. It gives Claude, ChatGPT or Gemini your program, your current weights and your last four weeks of training, so it can work around a sore shoulder, plan a fortnight with only dumbbells, or rebuild a lift that has stalled.

That works because Trackload writes your training in a small plain-text plan language that any AI can read and write. The AI suggests the change, the program’s rules decide every next weight, and nothing moves until you approve it. Trackload makes no AI calls of its own: it’s your AI, on your account.

Progression is built in.

Whoever writes the plan, the app decides every next weight by the program's rules.

  • Hit the target The weight goes up.
  • Miss it The weight holds.
  • Keep missing It deloads, and builds back past the old best.
