Changing your plan with an LLM

Which LLM does Trackload work with?

Whichever chat assistant you already use. Trackload does not connect to one: Copy for LLM on the Build tab puts your request on the clipboard with your plan, the rules of the plan language, your current weights and your last four weeks of training. Paste that into the assistant, then copy its whole reply and tap Paste reply.

The brief explains the plan language in full, so an assistant that follows written instructions can answer without being taught anything else.

What can I ask for?

A new program from scratch, or changes to the one you run: swap an exercise, change sets and reps, train a different number of days, change how a lift progresses. Weights can be overridden too: one session lighter, a weight held until a date, a training max reset, or a deload now.

A change to one of the twelve built-in programs lands on your own copy of it. The library's version stays as it was.

The reply has an error in it

Trackload lists every line it could not read, with the reason, and applies nothing. Tap Copy error for LLM and paste that back into the same conversation: it carries the errors and the plan they were found in, so the assistant can fix just those lines.

Does anything I logged change?

No. A change reaches the plan and the weights you are about to lift, never a session you have already logged. Nothing at all changes until you tap Apply on the review.

What counts as an LLM change?

Applying a pasted reply that changes something counts once. A reply with errors, a reply that changes nothing, and a review you discard cost nothing. Edits you make by hand in the app never count.

Applying a program.txt edit from a Mac counts as an LLM change too, because the app cannot tell an assistant's edit from your own. The Changed on your Mac card says so before you apply.

How do I use it from a Mac?

Turn on iCloud backup in Settings. A Trackload folder appears in your iCloud Drive with the program you run as program.txt, your current weights as lifts.csv and your history as CSV. Edit program.txt, or let an assistant on your Mac that can open files edit it, then open Trackload on your phone: the edit waits on the Build tab as Changed on your Mac, with the same review a pasted reply gets.

Pasting from the phone needs no iCloud at all. Only the folder does.

I bought the unlock on another phone

Settings › Restore purchase, or Restore purchase on the unlock screen. The purchase belongs to your Apple Account, so it comes back on any phone signed in to it.

Training

How does the app decide my next weight?

Every program runs one of six progression schemes, and the app never hides which. On the Analytics tab, "How these numbers are derived" explains the estimated one-rep max, the training max and the intensity bands. In a session, the weight shown is already rounded to the smallest pair of plates the lift can use.

A lift has stalled. What now?

The app holds the weight and says so. That is the scheme working: a linear progression that keeps adding weight after a miss is how people get hurt. If it keeps happening on the same lift, the program may have run its course — most linear programs have a shelf life of a few months by design. You can also ask an LLM to change the lift, or deload it, and see the change before it applies.

Can I edit a program?

Yes. On the Build tab, Make a copy to edit turns one of the twelve programs into your own, and Edit by hand changes days, exercises, sets, reps, the progression rule, starting weights, the name and the schedule. Editing by hand is free and unlimited.

Settings and data

How do I change between kilograms and pounds?

Settings › Units. Changing units relabels your existing weights rather than converting them: a 100 kg squat becomes a 100 lb squat. This is deliberate — the alternative silently rewrites your training history — so pick a unit before you start logging.

What happens to my data if I delete the app?

With iCloud backup off, it goes with the app. With iCloud backup on, your log stays in your own iCloud container and comes back when you reinstall, and the Trackload folder stays in iCloud Drive. See the privacy policy for how to remove both.

Can I export my training?

With iCloud backup on, the Trackload folder in iCloud Drive holds every set and session you have logged as CSV, and your current weights, updated as you train. Finished sessions can also go to Health.

How do I get my sessions into Health?

Settings › Write sessions to Health, then grant permission when iOS asks. Finished sessions appear in Fitness as strength training workouts. Sessions logged before you turned it on are not backfilled.

Do I need a paid developer account for iCloud backup?

No. That question comes up because iCloud sync needs a paid account on the developer's side to build. As a user you need nothing but an Apple Account signed in on your iPhone, and iCloud Drive switched on.

Which heart rate straps work?

Any standard Bluetooth LE chest strap. An Apple Watch is not used: HealthKit will not let one app save another app's readings, so a watch's heart rate belongs to the watch's own workout. Heart rate is entirely optional and everything else works without it.

Known limitations

  • iPhone only, portrait only. No iPad layout and no Apple Watch app.
  • Barbell strength only. No cardio, no recovery index, no readiness number.
  • One program at a time. You can switch, and your history is kept, but you cannot run two in parallel.
  • Trackload does not talk to an LLM itself. Every change goes through the clipboard or the Mac folder, and through the review.
  • Changing units relabels rather than converts, as above.
  • Estimated one-rep max is not calculated above twelve reps, because the formulae are fitted on low-rep work and a number derived from a set of twenty is not worth charting.
